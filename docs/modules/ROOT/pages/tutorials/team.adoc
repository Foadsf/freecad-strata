= Work as a team
:description: Share a project through a network folder, synced drive or Git server, open a colleague's project, sync and resolve concurrent changes.

*Goal:* Ada shares the Gearbox project; Bob opens it, both work and sync.

== Choose a shared location

Any of these works:

Network folder:: for example `\\server\cad\Gearbox` or a mounted share.
Synced drive:: a folder in OneDrive, Nextcloud, Dropbox or similar.
Git server:: an address such as `https://git.example.com/cad/Gearbox.git` on GitHub, GitLab, Gitea or your company's server. Git's normal sign-in applies; on Windows the Git Credential Manager asks once.

For a folder, Strata prepares it the first time. If the folder already contains other files, Strata creates a sub-folder `Gearbox.strata` in it.

== Share a project

[.steps]
. Click btn:[Share...] in the panel footer, or press kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+Y].
. Enter or browse to the shared location.
. Click btn:[Share and sync].

image::screenshots/12_share.png[The Share project dialog,role=screenshot,width=520]

The footer now reads _Shared · synced just now_.

== Open a colleague's project

[.steps]
. Open the panel's btn:[⋯] menu and choose *Open a shared project...*.
. Enter the shared location, and an empty folder for your copy.
. Click btn:[Open project].

image::screenshots/18_join.png[The Open shared project dialog,role=screenshot,width=560]

Strata copies the project with its complete history, versions and releases, and opens its documents.

== Sync

Click btn:[Sync now] in the footer, or press kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+Y], whenever you want to exchange work:

* your new history, versions, releases and workspaces are sent;
* your colleagues' are fetched. If only they changed your current workspace, your documents are updated and reloaded;
* if you both changed it, Strata merges automatically as long as you changed *different* documents;
* if you both changed the *same* document, Strata asks whether to review and merge now, and opens the merge dialog. See xref:tutorials/workspaces.adoc#merge-the-alternative-into-main[Merge].

Colleagues' new workspaces appear in the workspace menu marked _(from team)_.

== Good habits

* Sync before you start and when you finish a piece of work.
* Agree who works on which document at a time. Strata has no file locking yet, so two people editing the same document leads to a merge choice.
* Use workspaces for longer experiments, so Main stays clean for everybody.

NOTE: History stays on your computer until you sync. If you never share, nothing leaves your computer.
