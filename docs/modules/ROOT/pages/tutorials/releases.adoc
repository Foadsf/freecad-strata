= Release a design
:description: Release a version under a part number and revision, use the approval step, reject and resubmit, and choose a revision scheme.

*Goal:* release version V2 of the bracket and shaft for manufacturing, have the release approved, then revise the bracket.

This tutorial follows Onshape's https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Release/release_management.htm[Release management] help page: a release candidate is created from a version, goes through *Pending*, and ends *Released* or *Rejected*.

== Before you start: part numbers

Give the documents part numbers first; xref:tutorials/properties.adoc[Part numbers and properties] shows how to do it for all documents at once.
A release reads them from the document *as it was at the released version*, so set them before you create the version.

== Release a version

[.steps]
. Right-click the version in the graph and choose *Release V2...*, or select it and click btn:[Release] in the details card.
. Tick the documents to release. For each, check the *part number* and the *revision*.
. Optionally edit the description.
. Click btn:[Release].

image::screenshots/10_release.png[Releasing version V2,role=screenshot,width=700]

The part number and description come from the document's properties at that version, else from earlier releases of the same document. A document without a part number starts unticked.
The revision is the next free one for that part number in the project's revision scheme.

The version's node in the graph now carries a green chip such as *BRK-100 A*.

== Require approval

Tick *Require approval before it is released*. The button becomes btn:[Submit for approval] and the revision is created as *Pending*, with an amber chip.

[.steps]
. The approver opens *Releases* (kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+R]) and selects the pending row.
. btn:[Approve] makes it *Released* and records the approver's name.
. btn:[Reject] asks for a reason, for example _Hole pattern does not match drawing_, and makes it *Rejected*.

== Fix and resubmit a rejected revision

A rejected revision does not use up its letter.

[.steps]
. Fix the design and create a new version, for example _V2.1_.
. Release it. Strata proposes the same revision again, here *A*.
. Submit it for approval as before.

The register keeps both attempts: the rejected one with its reason, and the new pending one. Only the newest attempt counts for the revision's state.

== The release register

*Releases* lists every part number and revision with its state, document, version, who requested and who approved it, and the comment given with the last state change.

image::screenshots/11_releases.png[The release register,role=screenshot,width=860]

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| btn:[Approve], btn:[Reject] | for pending revisions
| btn:[Obsolete...] | for a released revision that is no longer valid; see xref:tutorials/revision-history.adoc[Revision history and obsolete revisions]
| btn:[View] | opens the released document as a read-only copy
| btn:[Export...] | writes the released document as `.FCStd` and `.step` into a folder, for a supplier or an archive
|===

Releases are permanent: each state change is recorded separately and cannot be edited afterwards.

== Choose a revision scheme

Open btn:[⋯] › *Settings...* and pick a *Revision scheme*:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| A, B, C ... | letters as in ASME Y14.35: I, O, Q, S, X and Z are skipped, and after Y comes AA (default)
| 1, 2, 3 ... | numbers
|===

image::screenshots/17_settings.png[Project settings with the revision scheme,role=screenshot,width=560]

The scheme applies to the next revision of every part number in the project; existing revisions keep their labels.
