= Explore an alternative
:description: Create a workspace for a design alternative, switch between workspaces, and merge the result into Main.

*Goal:* try a lighter bracket without disturbing the main design, then adopt it.

*You need:* a project with at least one version.

== Create a workspace

[.steps]
. Click the workspace pill btn:[Main ▾] and choose *New workspace...*. To start from an earlier point instead, right-click a version or history entry and choose *Branch to create workspace...*.
. Give it a name, for example _Lightweight_, and optionally a description.
. Click btn:[Create workspace].

You are now in *Lightweight*. The pill, the status bar and the history header all say so, and the graph has a new lane.

image::screenshots/03_workspace_menu.png[The workspace menu,role=screenshot,width=220]

== Work in the workspace

Edit and save as usual, for example make the base plate 6 mm instead of 12 mm. Create versions here too, for example _LW-1 – Lightweight option for review_.

image::screenshots/08_workspace.png[Working in the Lightweight workspace,role=screenshot]

== Switch between workspaces

Choose another workspace from the pill's menu, or right-click its node in the graph and choose *Switch to ...*.
Strata saves your work first if you agree, rewrites the documents that differ and reloads them in FreeCAD.
Main still has the 12 mm plate; Lightweight has the 6 mm one.

NOTE: Opening an assembly can make FreeCAD mark it as modified, because it refreshes external links. Strata then asks whether to save first. After a switch, Strata clears that flag for documents you did not edit.

== Merge the alternative into Main

[.steps]
. Switch to *Main*.
. Open the pill's menu and choose *Merge into Main > Lightweight*. Or right-click the Lightweight node and choose *Merge Lightweight into Main*.
. Review the merge dialog, then click btn:[Merge].

image::screenshots/09_merge.png[The merge dialog,role=screenshot,width=600]

Documents changed only in Lightweight are taken, documents changed only in Main are kept, and you choose for documents changed in both.
The merge appears in history as *Merged Lightweight into Main*, and a banner offers btn:[Undo merge] right afterwards.
xref:tutorials/merging.adoc[Merge workspaces] covers merge strategies, conflicts and undo step by step.

== Delete a workspace

Right-click the workspace's node in the graph and choose *Delete workspace...*. Its versions are kept. Main cannot be deleted, and you cannot delete the workspace you are in.
